My LibreSpeed behind Caddy showed ~40 Mbps on a 100 Mbps line. Fixed it with:  HTTP/1.1 instead of HTTP/2 (Caddy turns HTTP/2 on by default) default grace times (I had set them to 0) BBR on the server
